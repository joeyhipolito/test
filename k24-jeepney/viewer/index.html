Stage 1 of 5 · Skin

Stainless skin

Mirror-polished 304 panels hand-formed over the cell and bolted to it, the way a Sarao body hangs on a frame. Chrome bumper and overriders, slotted grille, sun-visor eyebrow, three air horns, rear step and a swing-out spare.

Stage 2 of 5 · Cabin

Cabin

Two forward-facing benches behind bucket seats. Every seat gets a three-point belt on anchors welded to the cell. The dash beam and the driver airbag come across from the donor.

Stage 3 of 5 · Powertrain

Honda K24

A 2.4-litre DOHC i-VTEC inline-four, transverse on the donor's front subframe with a six-speed manual. 4-2-1 header, aluminium radiator, standalone ECU on the firewall. The fuel tank sits inside the cell ahead of the rear axle.

Stage 4 of 5 · Running gear

Running gear

MacPherson struts and aluminium lower arms on a bolt-in front subframe. Multi-link rear on its own subframe. Vented discs and ABS at all four corners, all carried over from the donor. Drag the view under the floor to see it.

Stage 5 of 5 · Safety cell

Boron cell

What the skin hides. The donor's zoned body: boron steel pillars, roof rails and sills, extra-high-strength bows and crash boxes, high-strength rails, mild steel where it should crumple. This is why a cheap donor can still be a safe one.
